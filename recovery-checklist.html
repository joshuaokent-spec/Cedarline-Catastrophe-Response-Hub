Severe Weather Recovery Checklist

Safety first. Do not enter unsafe structures, approach downed electrical lines, drive through floodwater, or put yourself at risk to document damage.

When it is safe

Photograph visible damage from stable, safe locations.
Write down the approximate time and what happened.
Record emergency or incident report numbers you already have.
Keep receipts for reasonable emergency mitigation or temporary lodging.
List the most obvious damaged rooms, areas or items.
Save contractor or mitigation contact information as work begins.

What can wait

A perfect inventory of every damaged item.
Long-term repair estimates.
Final coverage conclusions.
Organizing every receipt before reporting the loss.

Actual coverage is determined by policy terms, limits, exclusions and endorsements. Follow local emergency instructions.
