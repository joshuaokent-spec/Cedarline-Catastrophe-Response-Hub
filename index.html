Recovery support

What can wait until the immediate danger passes?

01

Perfect damage inventory

You do not need a complete room-by-room inventory before starting a claim. Begin with what you can safely observe.

02

Repair estimates

Emergency mitigation and long-term repair planning are different tasks. Do not delay safety decisions while trying to price the entire loss.

03

Every receipt

Keep receipts as recovery continues, but do not put yourself back into danger to retrieve paperwork or belongings.

04

Coverage conclusions

A report can be submitted before coverage is determined. Policy terms, limits, exclusions and endorsements still govern the claim.

Visual design rationale

A more urgent visual language without turning the experience into an alarm screen

Storm navy#102F38

Creates a distinct catastrophe context and supports light-on-dark hero content.

Safety yellow#F5C451

Reserved for immediate-attention guidance and focus—not routine insurance actions.

Cedar green#0B4A43

Returns the user to familiar Cedarline insurance actions once safety is established.

Recovery mist#EEF4F2

Softens later-stage content and visually signals a transition out of immediate danger.
